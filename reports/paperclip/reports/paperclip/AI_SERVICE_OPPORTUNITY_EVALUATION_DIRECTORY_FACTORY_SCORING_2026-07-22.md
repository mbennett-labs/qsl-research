# 7. Revenue and Validation Principles

## 7.1 Sell the Audit Before the Full Build

A paid audit reduces risk for both parties.

It gives the client:

- a useful artifact,
- a defined problem,
- a practical roadmap,
- and a basis for approving implementation.

It gives QSL:

- evidence,
- reusable patterns,
- discovery data,
- and a path to Invoice #0001.

---

## 7.2 Prove One Measurable Outcome

Each engagement should define one principal business result, such as:

- reduced intake time,
- fewer missed leads,
- faster reporting,
- fewer repetitive steps,
- higher data completeness,
- improved visibility,
- reduced error rate,
- or faster human review.

Avoid engagements whose value is defined only as “using more AI.”

---

## 7.3 Preserve Human Control

The service should differentiate itself through governed automation:

- humans approve consequential actions,
- evidence is retained,
- uncertainty is visible,
- external actions are logged,
- automation boundaries are explicit,
- and rollback is planned.
