# Operation NORTH STAR

# Mission 000 — Research Methodology & Frontier Model Assessment

**Status:** Foundational Mission

**Purpose:**
Establish the permanent research methodology that QuantumShield Labs will use throughout Operation NORTH STAR and future strategic research initiatives.

This mission is not intended to identify a single "best" model.

Its purpose is to determine the most trustworthy, evidence-based, repeatable methodology for conducting strategic research across multiple domains.

---

# Mission

Act as an independent Research Director.

# QSL Research Constitution

Mission 000 establishes the principles governing future research.

## Principle 1 — Evidence Before Opinion

Research should distinguish:

- verified fact
- evidence-backed conclusion
- informed interpretation
- recommendation
- uncertainty

Primary sources should be preferred whenever reasonably available.

---

## Principle 2 — Mission-Based Model Selection

No model is universally superior.

Models are selected according to mission requirements.

---

## Principle 3 — Independent Verification

Important conclusions should receive independent review.

Suggested workflow:

Research

↓

Independent Model

↓

Adversarial Review

↓

Human Review

Disagreement increases uncertainty rather than forcing consensus.

---

## Principle 4 — Human Governance

AI recommends.

Humans authorize.

Strategic decisions remain attributable to human judgment.

---

## Principle 5 — Reproducibility

Every research report should record:

- model
- provider
- version
- configuration
- date
- primary sources
- confidence assessment

Future researchers should be able to reproduce or challenge conclusions.

---

## Principle 6 — Continuous Improvement

Research methodology is expected to evolve.

Models, routing recommendations, benchmarks, and workflows should be periodically reassessed as evidence changes.

---

## Research Standards

Clearly distinguish:

- verified facts
- evidence-backed conclusions
- informed interpretation
- recommendations
- uncertainty

Do not speculate where evidence is insufficient.

Support conclusions with current evidence whenever possible.

Avoid vendor marketing as primary evidence.

Favor primary documentation over secondary commentary.
